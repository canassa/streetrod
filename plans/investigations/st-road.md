# Road, race and scenery records

Investigation on branch `st-road`, 2026-11-05. The thirteen requested records
were investigated in the order below, before editing their declarations.
Evidence pins in the investigation sections refer to the pre-edit source at
`3848b38`; the final spelling map below makes renamed fields traceable.

## 1. RoadEdges (RACE.H)

Purpose: the rival's lookahead left road boundary and the mirror's outer verges.
Storage: communal `g_edgesRoad` in RACE.C:899, not serialized. ROAD.C writes it;
VIEW.C borrows its array elements through `g_renderRows`. No resource loader.
Layout: 34 bytes, signed words at 0, 2..16 and 18..32.

| Member | Meaning, values and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| leftEdge | World X at player sample +4, either 1500 or 2000 according to width flags (ROAD.C:945-959). Not the current collision edge. RACE.C:985,1265-1346 averages it with RoadView.rightEdge to choose the rival lane. |
| leftOuterX[8] | Mirror screen-pixel X of world X 1100, nearest row first; ROAD.C:829,849,892 writes through p42, VIEW.C:1215,1222 consumes indices 7 and 6 then walks toward 0. |
| rightOuterX[8] | Mirror screen-pixel X of world X 3700, same row order; ROAD.C:830,848,890 and VIEW.C:1216,1223. |

Only the boundary has a two-value domain, and those values are coordinates,
not modes: terrain flags select them. Do not enum-type a world coordinate.
Rename the boundary and mirror arrays to disclose their distinct roles.

## 2. BodyPoints (RACE.H)

Purpose: front/rear window and fuel-cap anchors for the displayed car.
Storage: communal `g_featurePos` in RACE.C:101. CARPIC.C:295-307 loads per-model
`g_pointsModelService` values, then CARPIC.C:496-506 translates them by the car's
screen origin. Runtime derived state, not a saved-game record.
Layout: 14 bytes; seven signed words, in declaration order.

| Member | Meaning, values and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| hasRearGlass | Boolean: both rear anchor components were nonzero (CARPIC.C:305). Enables the rear cleaning hotspot (RACE.C:164-165). Use BOOL. |
| frontGlassX/frontGlassY | Window anchor pixels, initially car-relative, translated only when frontGlassX > 0. VIDEO.C:141 sets both to -1 as invalid markers. RACE.C:166 creates the front cleaning hotspot. |
| rearGlassX/rearGlassY | Optional rear window anchor pixels, translated in the same conditional as the front pair (CARPIC.C:502-503); hasRearGlass, not the translated coordinates, determines presence. |
| gasCapX/gasCapY | Cap anchor pixels, always translated (CARPIC.C:505-506); CARPIC.C:591-592 converts back to car-relative bitmap coordinates. RACE.C:144-200,265-268 positions hotspots, cap art and saved background rectangles. |

The glass click cases currently have no action (RACE.C:419-422). Document anchors,
not a working cleaning effect. Preserve negative coordinate sentinels.

## 3. RivalPace (RACE.H)

Purpose: mph targets used to restrain the rival or substituted police car.
Storage: private uninitialized `g_rivalPace` (RACE.C:61), regenerated by
`ra_reset_eng` (RACE.C:952-955), not loaded/saved. Four signed words, 8 bytes.

| Member | Meaning, values and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| topSpeed | Stock specification speed from pf_vmax; used as the curve-speed base before the racer's penalty (RACE.C:1085-1088). |
| speedMargin | 10 or 16 mph according to processor rating (RACE.C:955); added to player speed when within four samples, with a slowSpeed floor (1107-1111). A speed delta, not a mode. |
| fastSpeed | Truncated 80% of topSpeed, the faster target when >12 samples ahead (1096-1100). |
| slowSpeed | Truncated 70% of topSpeed, the slower lead target and nearby minimum (1101,1109). Police substitution changes this to 60%; fastSpeed stays 80% and topSpeed becomes the King's specification speed (1374-1376). |

These are ordinary signed speed quantities, not closed enum codes.

## 4. GasInteraction (RACE.H)

Purpose: gates for the rag, pump and cap input commands during refuelling.
Storage: private uninitialized `g_fuelStationActions` (RACE.C:42), rebuilt with
hotspots by the five `spots_*` helpers; no resource or save data. Three signed
words, 6 bytes, each a boolean. Use BOOL and TRUE/FALSE at every assignment and
explicit zero comparison; preserve existing truth-test style.

| Member | Readers and writers |
| --- | --- |
| capAvailable | Cleared by clear_gas_spots (114), set by spots_cap/ spots_nozzle (146,157), checked for KR_GAS_CAP (383). |
| ragAvailable | Cleared at 112, enabled by spots_idle/ spots_rag (136,170), checked for KR_GAS_RAG (339). |
| nozzleAvailable | Cleared at 113, enabled by spots_idle/ spots_nozzle (137,156), checked for KR_GAS_PUMP (357). |

Availability means an accepted command, including returning a held tool; it does
not mean the nozzle is on the pump or a rag is physically absent/present.

## 5. RivalPosition (RACE.H)

Purpose: longitudinal and lateral simulation position of the rival/police car.
Storage: communal `g_roadOppPos` in SYS.C:983, initialized by init_rival_eng
(RACE.C:983-985), then advanced by move_rival. Not serialized. 16 bytes: four
signed words then eight untouched bytes. Keep the spare member unchanged.

| Member | Meaning, value range and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| roadIndex | Track sample index; starts at RACE_START_INDEX, advances by roadFraction >> 3 (RACE.C:1250); avoidance can force it behind the player (1309,1317,1321), and collision handling can decrement it (1459). Read by pace, pursuit, finish statistics, SYS.C:1122 and VIEW.C:1079,1328,1501,1523. |
| roadFraction | Eighths of a sample, normalized 0..7 after positive movement (1248-1251); starts 0. Stats and collision compare combined index*8+fraction (568,1424-1425); VIEW.C:1080 uses relative eighths for sprite projection. |
| distanceStep | Eighths of a sample per movement update, computed from opponent speed by ra_speed_step (1245-1248). Not mph or elapsed ticks; slow hosts have a different speed-to-step mapping (1165-1210). Also used in passing/steering decisions and VIEW.C:1129. |
| lateralX | World X across the road; starts at the lookahead road midpoint minus 200 (985), approaches target by one eighth of the remaining difference (1349-1356). Used by collision, steering and sprite X projection (1427, ROAD.C:965, VIEW.C:1081,1142,1146). |
| unused[8] | No direct member reader or writer found; preserved spare storage. |

No enum for indices, fractions or speeds: these are numeric coordinates and
quantities. distanceStep is not initialized in init_rival_eng; do not claim it is.

## 6. Spot (RACE.H)

Purpose: one positioned blood overlay in the severe-crash sequence.
Storage: private initialized `g_crashPics[3]` at RACE.C:454-455. No mutation,
loader or serialization. Six bytes: picture ID, destination X, destination Y.

| Member | Meaning and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| id | PIC_CRASH_BLOOD_LEFT/CENTER/RIGHT passed to di_bm at RACE.C:545. Resource identifiers, not hotspot hit codes. |
| x | Left destination pixel: 64, 208, 272, copied to blit dx at 549. |
| y | Top destination row: 84, 82, 123, copied to blit dy at 550. |

Only used when speed exceeds TOTAL_LOSS_MPH, in table order with shrinking
retrace delays (RACE.C:543-553). Rename type to CrashOverlay and id to pictureId;
reuse existing PIC_* identifiers without introducing a duplicate picture enum.

## 7. MirrorEdges (ROAD.H)

Purpose: projected right road edge and base building-top rows for the mirror.
Storage: communal `g_mirGeom` (ROAD.C:623), regenerated by rd_project_behind.
32 bytes: two arrays of eight signed words. Not serialized.

| Member | Meaning and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| buildingY[8] | Screen-pixel projection of world Y 1900 (ROAD.C:831,851,895); written only for town or gantry samples (821-822,860). May retain stale values for other samples; readers only use applicable scenery (VIEW.C:1217,1221,1237-1239). Base projection, not necessarily the ultimate roof row. |
| rightX[8] | Mirror screen-pixel road edge, nearest row first; width flags choose world X 2800 or 3300 (ROAD.C:826,837-847,868-886). VIEW.C:1213,1219 borrows rows 7/6 and traverses toward 0. |

Left road X and road-surface Y live in separate globals. Do not imply this is
all mirror geometry or conflate the world-space lookahead boundary with it.

## 8. RoadView (ROAD.H)

Purpose: player's track position, view reference and lookahead right boundary.
Storage: communal `g_trackViewMotion` in SYS.C:984. rd_reset initializes runtime
state, rd_advance reads generated track arrays, movement and steering update it.
Not serialized. Nine signed words, 18 bytes, no layout changes.

| Member | Meaning, values and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| rightEdge | World X of right boundary at player sample +4; 2800 or 3300 (ROAD.C:945-959), paired with RoadEdges.leftEdge for rival lane selection. Rename lookaheadRightX. |
| roadIndex | Current track sample, initially 200 racing or 10/65/115 cruising according to departure scene (ROAD.C:692-708). rd_advance adds the carried eighths (926); drives road cursors, scenery, finish/pursuit and SYS.C:1122-1140. |
| roadFraction | Eighths within the current sample; reset 0, add distanceStep then carry/mask to 0..7 (ROAD.C:711,926-928,1051-1053; RACE.C:1224-1227). Controls near projected row distances (ROAD.C:767,857). |
| distanceStep | Eighths per movement update: reset 0; cruising 4 or 6 by CPU, racing ra_speed_step(speed) (ROAD.C:717,1033-1038; RACE.C:1224-1225). Used for lateral drift and comparison with rival. |
| lateralX | Player/view world X, reset 2560 (ROAD.C:712), changed by steering drift, tire failure and autopilot (969-973,1043-1049; RACE.C:1470). Used by both X projectors, roadside/rival collision and background rendering. |
| cameraY | Vertical world coordinate of the camera, reset 1900; no later writes. Subtracted from world Y in forward/mirror projection (ROAD.C:656,683). |
| unused | Reset 0 (ROAD.C:714), otherwise unread. Keep signed word. |
| curveX | View's horizontal curve reference, reset 0; steering changes it (ROAD.C:1022-1032), autopilot snaps it to the road profile (979), collision shakes it (RACE.C:1474). Compared with track curve samples for projection/drift and used for skyline scrolling (VIEW.C:297-328). Rename curveReference to avoid implying a literal lateral coordinate. |
| roadHeight | Current sample's height-profile value (ROAD.C:961), reset 0, subtracted against projected samples (743,773,833,863), also used to position skyline/rival (VIEW.C:294,1138). Numeric profile coefficient, not screen Y. |

## 9. Part (SCENERY.H)

Purpose: one horizontal colored span of a roadside sprite.
Storage: immutable far arrays g_spans1/g_spans2 emitted by SPANS1.ASM and
SPANS2.ASM's four-byte PART macro (not a disk-loaded bitmap). Accessed through
Run.parts by vw_sprite (VIEW.C:362-387); all four bytes are signed and remain so.

| Member | Meaning and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| dx | Pixel X offset from sprite anchor; negated in reflected drawing (VIEW.C:374,382). |
| dy | Pixel Y offset from anchor, normally nonpositive (376,384). |
| w | Endpoint separation in pixels, not pixel count: both span endpoints are inclusive in en_fill/cl_span/tl_fill. Zero draws one pixel (SPANS1.ASM:16-22, EGASPAN.ASM:65-90). Rename extent. |
| color | Logical drawing color index passed unchanged to fillSpan (377,385), interpreted by the active adapter. Preserve signed byte. The resource uses palette indices rather than stable named hues: VIDEO.C:66,68 swaps mappings, and CGA has its own conversion. |

Rename type ScenerySpan. The palette domain is shared rendering state, not a
new scenery-kind enum. Existing PaletteSlot names cover only paint registers;
using that partial enum as the full color domain or inventing hue names would
misdescribe these assets. Keep the numeric format and flag the missing complete
logical-color vocabulary below rather than renaming unrelated render colors.

## 10. Run (SCENERY.H)

Purpose: one pre-scaled scenery sprite represented by a counted span array.
Storage: initialized Run arrays in SCENERY.C:7-41 and SCENERY2.C:6-46,
public declarations/forward references retained in their existing order.
VIEW.C:370 copies one record locally. Six bytes: signed count then far pointer.

| Member | Meaning and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| n | Number of spans; j < run.n iterates every record (VIEW.C:373,381); actual tables range from 2 through 306. Rename spanCount. |
| parts | Far pointer into one of the two span pools, addressed in four-byte units; offsets and counts in both scenery source tables determine slices, including intentional reused rock slices. Rename spans. |

Rename type ScenerySprite. No ownership transfer, allocation, loader or saving.
The far qualifier is essential; the table itself and the local copy are near.

## 11. List (SCENERY.H)

Purpose: distance-ordered sizes of one scenery object.
Storage: initialized lists in SCENERY.C and SCENERY2.C; g_scenery provides
SceneryId-indexed pointers. Four bytes: signed count and near pointer.

| Member | Meaning and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| last | Count of sizes, exclusive bound; vw_sprite tests last > first (VIEW.C:369). Values 7 normally, 8 hydrant, 1 dashboard dot (SCENERY.C:6,12,40). Rename lodCount. |
| items | Near pointer to the size table; index 0 is smallest/farthest, increasing toward largest/nearest. render_object and render_hydrant map 9-distance to a clamped index (VIEW.C:911-931). Rename lods. |

Rename type SceneryLods. The three observed counts are lengths, not modes.
The API checks the upper bound only, so negative indices remain a caller error;
do not change behavior while documenting it. Dot uses index 0 (VIEW.C:906).

## 12. ViewPages (VIEW.H)

Purpose: swap the drawing/display roles of the two driving buffers.
Storage: initialized global g_viewPages = {1,2} (VIEW.C:68); flip_page swaps them
only in EGA mode (105-109). No loader/save. Four bytes, two signed words.

| Member | Meaning and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| draw | Driving page to render into: 1 selects EGA_PAGE1_SEGMENT / g_driveScreenB, 2 EGA_PAGE2_SEGMENT / g_bufferSceneA (VIEW.C:82,108,314 and subsequent composition calls). DRIVE.C:157-170 uses the opposite buffer to restore backgrounds. |
| show | Page passed to ep_show (VIEW.C:83,109); starts 2 while draw starts 1. Only swapped thereafter. |

Search found hardware segment/offset constants but no page-role enum. Introduce
DrivingPage for these two values, type both members and flip_page's swap local,
replace the initializer and every draw == 1 use in VIEW.C and DRIVE.C. This does
not retype ep_show: its other users can select page 0 and animation pages.

## 13. ViewClip (VIEW.H)

Purpose: screen-pixel clipping limits shared by view drawing and span backends.
Storage: global g_viewClip initialized {0,18,101,319} in VIEW.C:70. vw_clip restores
that rectangle; clip_mirror uses {242,22,42,317}; vw_unclip_top changes only top
to 0 (VIEW.C:163-185). DASH.C:511-517,564-572 temporarily borrows vertical limits
for dashboard drawing. No save or loader. Four signed words, 8 bytes, in the
unusual order xMin/yMin/yMax/xMax; assembler uses fixed offsets 0/2/4/6.

| Member | Meaning and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| xMin | Inclusive left screen pixel (VIEW.C:142-145,153; EGASPAN.ASM:71-85). |
| yMin | Inclusive top row for spans/lines and blit clipping (VIEW.C:121-124,153-157; EGASPAN.ASM:65-66). |
| yMax | Inclusive bottom for spans/lines (EGASPAN.ASM:67-68; CGALINE.ASM:378; TANDYLIN.ASM:324), but exclusive end when clipping bitmap heights (VIEW.C:126-127,140-141). Preserve this existing one-row discrepancy. |
| xMax | Inclusive right pixel for spans/lines (VIEW.C:153; EGASPAN.ASM:72); sprite bounding-box rejection uses dx < xMax (VIEW.C:1187). |

Also used by fill-below backends (EGASPAN.ASM:167-179, CGALINE.ASM:503-514,
TANDYLIN.ASM:419-433) and fill_quad's row limit (VIEW.C:485). These differing
consumer contracts rule out promising a universally half-open rectangle.

## Other misleading names and open questions (outside scope)

- ra_speed_step's argument is named rpm, but callers pass DrivingState.speed;
  it maps mph to eighths of a sample (RACE.C:1163-1210,1225,1245). Do not rename
  that function's parameter in this task.
- SPANS1.ASM and SPANS2.ASM call the fourth PART macro argument h although it
  is the color byte; the third argument is inclusive endpoint separation rather
  than a pixel count. Macro parameter renaming is outside the structs' scope.
- g_featurePos sounds like a persistent model coordinate table; its coordinates
  transition to screen anchors and are invalidated by video initialization.
- g_yBuildingTopArray and MirrorEdges.buildingY store a base projection, not all
  finished roof heights; gantries and raised roofs derive their own coordinates.
- g_rivalLeft and g_edgesRoad do not denote screen-left mirror geometry in all
  contexts; lookahead world boundaries and projected mirror arrays are separate.
- No physical-distance conversion for a track sample was established. Describe
  eighths of samples, not meters/feet. CPU-dependent movement mapping prevents
  inferring a universal duration or velocity conversion from one distanceStep.
- Full logical color names remain a rendering-wide investigation: PaletteSlot
  only names mutable car-paint slots; no universal hue is implied by Part.color.
- ViewClip's bottom row has deliberately documented differing consumers; fixing
  that discrepancy would change behavior and is outside this investigation.

### Anchor resource provenance and picture-ID type follow-up

DISK.C:69-82's data-block table loads the sixth block of HOT_DATA into the
26-element g_pointsModelService array: 156 bytes, six unsigned coordinate bytes
per Spr6 (DISK.H:413-415). di_load_data reads each whole block in sequence
(DISK.C:501-527). The service block starts at file byte 1748, after the five
preceding blocks. In orig/freeware/HOT_DATA, fuelCapX/fuelCapY ranges are 0..85
and 0..36; rearGlassX/rearGlassY 0..76 and 0..6; frontGlassX/frontGlassY 0..176
and 0..10. Record 25 is entirely zero. Other records can have rearGlassY zero
with a nonzero X, confirming that both components are needed for hasRearGlass.
CARPIC promotes these bytes into the signed-word BodyPoints anchors before
screen translation. VIDEO's negative marker is runtime state, not file data.

Final header review also found the existing enum PictureId in DISK.H:10.
CrashOverlay.pictureId uses that enum and RACE.H includes its owner header;
there is no new picture enum or duplicate resource-ID set. The three table
initializers already use the proper PIC_* enumerators. The final layout probe
also verifies PictureId's two-byte size and signedness.
